Freight breaks down when it's split across too many vendors. Freightline exists to be the one team that owns your freight from booking to proof of delivery.

Our story

Started by people tired of chasing status updates.

Freightline started with a simple complaint we kept hearing from shippers: nobody could tell them, in one call, where their freight actually was. Every mode had its own vendor, its own portal, its own definition of "on time." We built Freightline to collapse that into a single relationship — one team, one dashboard, one number to call — without giving up the specialist carriers and brokers who actually move the freight well.

Today that same principle runs through every shipment we touch: proactive updates before you have to ask, and a team that treats your delivery date like it's their own deadline.

What we believe

Accountability, not hand-offs

One team owns your shipment end-o-end — we don't disappear at the mode boundary.

Visibility before you ask

You hear about a delay from us first, with a plan attached, not a status page you refresh.

Plain, direct communication

No jargon walls. If something's wrong, you'll know exactly what and what we're doing about it.
